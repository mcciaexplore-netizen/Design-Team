About the award

Instituted by Mr. Baburaoji Parkhe of the Parkhe Group in memory of his younger brother, G. S. Parkhe, the award was established to encourage innovation and new product development.

Presented since 1947, it recognises innovative products, processes, designs, services and import-substitute solutions that demonstrate market relevance and acceptance.

Key highlights

  • Presented since 1947 with a legacy of 70+ years
  • Oldest industrial award in India
  • Recognises innovation and new product development
  • 400+ entrepreneurs recognised to date
  • Follows a stringent selection process
  • Recognises innovations demonstrating market acceptance and industrial relevance

How your entry is evaluated

Every MCCIA award follows the same selection path. Plan for all six stages when you prepare your submission.

  1. 01

    Application Submission

    Applicants submit the completed form along with product catalogues and photographs to showcase their work and share their achievements.

  2. 02

    Shortlisting

    Every submission is carefully reviewed by our expert jury, with the most deserving candidates shortlisted for the next stage.

  3. 03

    Additional Documentation

    Shortlisted applicants are requested to provide further details such as catalogues, sales literature, applicant's bio-data and product photographs.

  4. 04

    Interview Invitation

    Shortlisted candidates get their chance to shine before the Selection Committee, presenting their case and showcasing the achievements that set their business apart.

  5. 05

    Further Evaluation

    The committee may seek additional information, documents or discussions before finalising awardees.

  6. 06

    Works / Factory Visit

    Where necessary, our Selection Committee visits the workplace to witness your work and excellence first-hand, providing an opportunity for a closer evaluation.

After careful evaluation and deliberation, the jury finalises the awardees.
