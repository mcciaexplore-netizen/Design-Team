About the award

Exports play an important role in India's economic growth, with Pune and Maharashtra being significant contributors to the country's export ecosystem. In line with its efforts to promote the region as a hub for investment and exports, MCCIA instituted the S. L. Kirloskar Export Excellence Awards to recognise outstanding export performance among its member companies.

The award recognises manufacturer-exporters across different scales of enterprise, celebrating their contribution to international trade and export-led growth.

Key highlights

  • Open to MCCIA members only
  • Recognises manufacturer-exporters
  • Three categories: small, medium and large scale
  • Selection based on export value for FY 2024–25
  • Export performance from the preceding two years is also considered

Award focus

Recognition for outstanding export performance among MCCIA member companies, across small, medium and large scale manufacturer-exporters.

Eligibility

Open to MCCIA members only. Manufacturer exporters in small, medium and large scale categories.

Awards are given to manufacturer-exporters in three categories:

  • Small Scale
  • Medium Scale
  • Large Scale

How your entry is evaluated

Every MCCIA award follows the same selection path. Plan for all six stages when you prepare your submission.

  1. 01

    Application Submission

    Applicants submit the completed form along with product catalogues and photographs to showcase their work and share their achievements.

  2. 02

    Shortlisting

    Every submission is carefully reviewed by our expert jury, with the most deserving candidates shortlisted for the next stage.

  3. 03

    Additional Documentation

    Shortlisted applicants are requested to provide further details such as catalogues, sales literature, applicant's bio-data and product photographs.

  4. 04

    Interview Invitation

    Shortlisted candidates get their chance to shine before the Selection Committee, presenting their case and showcasing the achievements that set their business apart.

  5. 05

    Further Evaluation

    The committee may seek additional information, documents or discussions before finalising awardees.

  6. 06

    Works / Factory Visit

    Where necessary, our Selection Committee visits the workplace to witness your work and excellence first-hand, providing an opportunity for a closer evaluation.

After careful evaluation and deliberation, the jury finalises the awardees.
