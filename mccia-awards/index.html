Award selection process

From application to award — the six steps

Every category follows the same evaluation path. Knowing it in advance is the easiest way to build a strong application.

  1. 01

    Application Submission

    Applicants submit the completed form along with product catalogues and photographs.

  2. 02

    Shortlisting

    The expert jury reviews submissions and shortlists the most deserving candidates.

  3. 03

    Additional Documentation

    Shortlisted applicants provide further details, sales literature, and bio-data.

  4. 04

    Interview Invitation

    Shortlisted applicants present to the Selection Committee.

  5. 05

    Further Evaluation

    The committee seeks additional information or discussions before finalising.

  6. 06

    Works / Factory Visit

    The Selection Committee visits the workplace for an on-the-spot evaluation where necessary.

After careful evaluation and deliberation, the jury finalises the awardees.

About the jury

Assessed by industry, not by algorithm

Every submission is reviewed by MCCIA's expert jury. Entries are assessed against the focus of the award applied for, and the most deserving candidates are shortlisted for the next stage.

Shortlisted applicants are invited to present their case before the Selection Committee for the award. The committee may seek additional information, documents or discussions, and may visit the works or factory for an on-the-spot study where it considers this essential, before finalising the awardees.

Before you apply

The awards recognise eight areas of enterprise: innovation and entrepreneurship, women entrepreneurship, exports and international business, sustainability and green initiatives, corporate social responsibility, defence manufacturing, agriculture, dairy and food processing, and first-generation entrepreneurship. Read what each award recognises below and apply to every category your organisation genuinely qualifies for.

Eligibility is specific to each award. The Dr. R. J. Rathi Award for green initiatives is open to all small, medium and large-scale units in manufacturing and service industry. The B. G. Deshmukh IAS Award is open to small, medium and large-scale enterprises with CSR activity in education, health and hygiene, environment, safety, energy and water conservation, waste management or any social field. The S. L. Kirloskar Export Excellence Award is open to MCCIA members only. Open any award below for its exact eligibility.

A trophy and a certificate. The Late Kiran Natu Udyojakta Puraskar additionally carries a medal and a cash prize of Rs. 1 lakh.
